# Per-compound literature check — the six confirmed altermagnets

*Due-diligence pass, 2026-07-10. Searches for "<compound> altermagnet(ic)" plus
known experimental magnetism of each compound.*

## Novelty status

Web searches for altermagnetism claims on all six compounds return **no prior
altermagnet claim** for any of them. They are additionally absent from Gao et al.'s
50 (checked against their table) and from the MAGNDATA-derived gold-standard set
(different source database). Two items require a final check before submission
(full text paywalled here):

- **arXiv:2601.01617** — "Magnetic Structures Database from Symmetry-aided
  High-Throughput Calculations" (Jan 2026), reports 392 altermagnets. MUST grep
  its supplementary list for our six before claiming novelty in print.
- **Naka, Motome & Seo, npj Spintronics 3, 1 (2025), "Altermagnetic perovskites"**
  — theory of the perovskite family mechanism; skim for specific-compound overlap.

## Known experimental magnetism vs. our claimed configuration (IMPORTANT)

Our DFT confirms altermagnetism **in the compensated collinear AFM configuration
catalogued by the Materials Project**. For some compounds that configuration is
not the experimental ground state — this must be disclosed:

| Compound | Experimental situation | Impact on claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Sr₂FeWO₆** | AFM insulator, T_N ≈ 37 K — **consistent with our configuration**. | Strongest case. |
| **Ba₂FeMoO₆** | Family famous as **half-metallic ferrimagnets** (Sr₂FeMoO₆, T_C ≈ 330 K); Ba₂FeMoO₆ experimentally ferrimagnetic. | Our AFM configuration is an alternative/metastable order, not the observed ground state. Claim must read: "altermagnetic in its compensated AFM configuration," possibly stabilizable by strain/doping. |
| **Y₂Mo₂O₇** | Canonical frustrated pyrochlore **spin glass** (T_f ≈ 22 K); no long-range order observed. | Our ordered AFM configuration is idealized; the altermagnetic state would require relieving frustration (pressure/strain/field cooling). Disclose. |
| **SrLaCoO₄** | Layered K₂NiF₄ cobaltite; magnetism composition-sensitive (La₂₋ₓSrₓCoO₄ series shows AFM correlations at this doping). | Plausible; configuration-specific claim still advised. |
| **VOF₂** | Little experimental literature; MP-computed structure. | Synthesis/stability to be established. |
| **MgCu₂(O₂F)₂** | Little experimental literature; MP-computed structure. | Synthesis/stability to be established. |

**Manuscript action taken:** a "configuration-specific claims" caveat added to the
Discussion/Limitations; abstract wording kept to what DFT actually shows.

## Bottom line

Novelty holds on current evidence (pending the arXiv:2601.01617 SI check). The
scientifically honest framing is: six compounds whose *compensated AFM
configuration* is DFT-confirmed altermagnetic; for Sr₂FeWO₆ that configuration is
the experimental ground state, for Ba₂FeMoO₆/Y₂Mo₂O₇ it is a competing order.
